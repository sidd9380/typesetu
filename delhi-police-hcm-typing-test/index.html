The real exam pattern, at a glance

Duration
10 minutes
Passage
Printed sheet, 400+ words
Minimum speed
30 WPM (net)
Scoring
25 marks in final merit

The passage is given on paper (minimum 400 words / 2,000 strokes, English) and typed on screen. Below 30 net WPM you are disqualified, no matter how well the written exam went. There are no half mistakes — every mistake is a full mistake, and one word is deducted per mistake before your net speed is worked out.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum typing speed for Delhi Police HCM?

30 WPM net in the 10-minute test. Below that, you are disqualified regardless of written marks.

How many marks does the HCM typing test carry?

25 marks in the final merit, awarded by net-speed bands — from 10 marks at 30 WPM up to the full 25 marks at 51 WPM and above.

Is the passage on paper or on screen?

On paper — a printed sheet of at least 400 words / 2,000 strokes. TypeSetu's Paper View practises exactly this format.

Are half mistakes counted?

No. Every mistake is a full mistake; one word is deducted per mistake.
