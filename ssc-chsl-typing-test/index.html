Error limit
UR 7% · Reserved 10%

SSC counts mistakes in two grades: a word wrong as a whole is a full mistake, and a single character-level slip inside a word is a half mistake. Your error percentage is (Full + ½ × Half) ÷ words typed × 100, and it must stay within 7% for UR or 10% for reserved categories. Typing fast is only half the test — the error percentage decides.

Frequently asked questions

What typing speed is required in SSC CHSL?

35 WPM in English, over a passage of about 1,750 key depressions in 10 minutes.

What is the error limit in the CHSL typing test?

7% for UR and 10% for reserved categories — calculated as (full mistakes + half of half mistakes) ÷ words typed.

What is a half mistake?

A single character-level error inside a word — one letter wrong, added or missed. A word wrong as a whole is a full mistake.

Is the passage on paper or on screen?

On screen — the passage is displayed and you type it in the window below.
