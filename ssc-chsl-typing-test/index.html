The real exam pattern, at a glance

Duration
10 minutes
Passage
On screen, ~1,750 key depressions
Speed required
35 WPM (English)
Error limit
UR 7% · Reserved 10%

SSC counts mistakes in two grades: a word wrong as a whole is a full mistake, and a capitalisation, punctuation or spacing slip is a half mistake — a spelling error, even one letter, is a full mistake. Your error percentage is (Full + ½ × Half) ÷ words typed × 100, and it must stay within 7% for UR or 10% for reserved categories. Typing fast is only half the test — the error percentage decides.

Frequently asked questions

What typing speed is required in SSC CHSL?

35 WPM in English, over a passage of about 1,750 key depressions in 10 minutes.

What is the error limit in the CHSL typing test?

7% for UR and 10% for reserved categories — calculated as (full mistakes + half of half mistakes) ÷ words typed.

What is a half mistake?

A capitalisation, punctuation or spacing slip. A spelling error — even a single letter — is a full mistake, as are skipped and extra words.

Is the passage on paper or on screen?

On screen — the passage is displayed and you type it in the window below.
