Error limits

Unlike CHSL, DEST sets no WPM band — you finish the passage work in 15 minutes and keep errors within your category's ceiling. Mistakes are counted as full (a word wrong as a whole) and half (a single character-level slip inside a word), and the error percentage is (Full + ½ × Half) ÷ words typed × 100. For most posts the ceilings are 20% UR, 25% OBC/EWS and 30% for other categories.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SSC CGL DEST?

The Data Entry Speed Test — a qualifying skill test in SSC CGL where you type about 2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes, with the passage shown on screen.

What are the error limits in DEST?

For most posts: 20% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS and 30% for other categories, from full mistakes plus half of the half mistakes, over words typed.

Is there a minimum WPM in DEST?

No separate speed band — finishing the work in 15 minutes inside your error limit is the test.

Is DEST counted in merit?

No — it is qualifying in nature for the posts that require it.
