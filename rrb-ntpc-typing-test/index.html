The real exam pattern, at a glance

Duration
10 minutes (evaluated)
Minimum words
300 — or not evaluated
Speed required
30 WPM (English)
Editing
Backspace disabled

Before the evaluated test there is a 1-minute practice passage and a break. Scoring is unlike SSC's: mistakes within the first 5% of the words you type are ignored; after that allowance, every remaining mistake — full = 1, half = 0.5 — deducts 10 words from your total before speed is calculated: net speed = (words typed − final mistakes × 10) ÷ minutes. Careless starts are forgiven a little; careless typing after that is expensive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum word count in RRB NTPC CBTST?

300 words in 10 minutes. Below that, the test is not evaluated at all.

What speed is required?

30 WPM net in English, after mistake deductions.

How is net speed calculated?

Mistakes in the first 5% of words typed are ignored. Each remaining mistake (full = 1, half = 0.5) deducts 10 words: (words − mistakes × 10) ÷ minutes.

Is backspace allowed?

No — editing is disabled in the real CBTST, and TypeSetu's RRB tests lock it the same way.
